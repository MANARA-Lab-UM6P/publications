# MANARA Lab · UM6P College of Computing
**Multimodal AI, Networks, Analytics, Recommender Systems & Augmented Sensing Laboratory**  
University Mohammed VI Polytechnic (UM6P) · College of Computing · Benguerir, Morocco

---

## 🔭 About the Lab
MANARA Lab investigates the design and evaluation of **trustworthy, intelligent, and networked
systems**, with work in trustworthy AI, wireless networks, security and privacy, speech processing,
healthcare AI, and mobile crowdsensing. Our
research spans algorithmic foundations and real-world deployable systems, bridging AI methods
with networked and physical system constraints. We place a strong emphasis on open and
reproducible science.

---

## 🧭 Research Axes

| Axis | Topics |
|------|--------|
| 🤖 Trustworthy & Explainable AI | Explainable AI (XAI), trust and fairness in AI systems, blockchain-based and cross-domain recommender systems, authorship verification and stylometry |
| 📡 Wireless Networks & Networked Systems | 5G/6G communications, NOMA, IRS-assisted communications, UAV and LEO satellite communications, HARQ, Internet of Things (IoT), resource management in wireless networks |
| 🔐 Security, Privacy & Blockchain | Physical layer security, jamming detection, trust and reputation management, federated learning with privacy guarantees, cloud forensics, decentralized systems and smart contracts |
| 🎙️ Speech & Affective Computing | Speaker identification, speech emotion recognition, overlapping speech detection, audio-visual fusion under missing modalities |
| 🏥 Healthcare AI & Medical Imaging | Medical image and surgical video analysis, retinal disease detection, cardiovascular risk prediction, multimodal fusion for clinical decision support |
| 📱 Mobile Crowdsensing | Task allocation, location privacy, crowdsensing frameworks |
